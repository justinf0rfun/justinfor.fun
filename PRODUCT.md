## Platform

## Users

Potential collaborators, employers, product users, and fellow independent builders evaluating Justin's shipped work.

## Product Purpose

Justin's personal site introduces his work as an AI-era builder and demonstrates that he can take products from definition through design, engineering, deployment, and iteration.

## Positioning

The site is a concise, evidence-led portfolio built around real shipped products rather than generic capability claims.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The Projects surface currently covers two shipped projects: Huoban and GSON.
- Huoban is an open orchestration protocol at `0.1.0-alpha.0`; it must not be presented as a mature agent runtime.
- GSON is a live developer tool whose public product name is JSON Workbench.
- Justin built both projects independently, but the public Projects page should not display role or solo-builder attribution.
- Project claims must be verifiable from the live products. Do not invent usage, revenue, customer, or growth metrics.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Justin and the site's existing concise English voice.
- Preserve the established light, tactile folder interface and restrained visual system.
- Use official product branding and real product interfaces.

## Evidence on Hand

- Huoban: https://huoban.dev/
- GSON / JSON Workbench: https://gson.ing/
- Both projects have live product surfaces and official brand assets suitable for screenshots and previews.
- No public user, revenue, or customer metrics are available.

## Product Principles

- Show shipped work before making claims about ability.
- Keep product status and maturity precise.
- Let real interfaces carry the visual proof.
- Prefer concise project summaries over long case studies.
