---
title: Small products deserve serious systems
description: A personal product needs fewer rules than a large team, but the few rules it keeps should be deliberate and durable.
publishedAt: 2026-07-27
---

Small does not have to mean improvised.

## Keep the vocabulary small

A handful of dependable colors, spacing decisions, and motion patterns is more useful than a large token set nobody remembers.

## Build around the recurring decision

The right abstraction is the one that removes a choice you keep making. Everything else can remain local until the repetition becomes real.

## Consistency creates room for personality

When the quiet parts behave predictably, the expressive parts can take more risk without making the whole product feel accidental.
